# Countries of origin: incoming laws (2026-11-18)

`laws-2026-11-18.csv` has 16 rows for Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh, in the CONTRIBUTING.md law columns plus `site_status`.

- 8 rows are **link upgrades**: the law is already in `laws-policies.csv`, which points only to the portal's front page. The new link opens the law itself.
- 8 rows are **new**: Nepal's Foreign Employment Tribunal Rules 2068, Trafficking Rules 2065 and National Policy against Trafficking in Persons 2082; Pakistan's Prevention of Smuggling of Migrants Act 2018; Bangladesh's Overseas Employment and Migrants (Amendment) Acts 2023 and 2026, the Human Trafficking and Migrant Smuggling Prevention and Suppression Act 2026, and the repealed Emigration Ordinance 1982.
- **Needs an editor:** Laws of Bangladesh marks the 2012 Prevention and Suppression of Human Trafficking Act as repealed. The site still lists it as current; the 2026 Act appears to replace it.
- Sources: lawcommission.gov.np, pakistancode.gov.pk, bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd (all official). Links found with each portal's own search on 2026-11-18. `adopted` is the year in the title only; in-force dates and coverage still have to be read from the texts, so every row is Unverified.
- Not reached this time: India Code (site migration), peraturan.bpk.go.id (Cloudflare), officialgazette.gov.ph (Cloudflare), vbpl.vn (search did not respond), slbfe.lk.
