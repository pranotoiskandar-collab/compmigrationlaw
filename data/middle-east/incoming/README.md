## Sources and how to read `source_tier`

- **official**: the government's own site (Lebanon Ministry of Justice; Israel Population and Immigration Authority on gov.il).
- **UN-hosted copy (UNODC SHERLOC)**: the text as filed with the UN Office on Drugs and Crime legislation database. This is a UN site, not the national gazette. Use it as a reliable copy and replace with the gazette link when an editor finds one.

## Not found from the cloud or the browser

Syria, Yemen and Iran have no official law database that could be reached, and the main texts for Egypt (Asylum Law 2024, Labour Law 2025), Iraq (Labour Law 37/2015) and Israel (Knesset texts) sit behind bot checks or failed to load. No court decisions were reachable for these countries. All of these are listed in `../../pacific/incoming/unreachable-portals-2026-11-20.md` and in the follow-up doc for a person to look up.

Descriptive only: rows record what the law says, with no ranking or assessment.
