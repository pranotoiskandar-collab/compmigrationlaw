The views were found in the OHCHR Jurisprudence database (juris.ohchr.org) with its issue filters: non-refoulement, extradition, refugee status, nationality and aliens' rights. Of the 62 non-Western hits, these 15 were not on the site yet. Each link goes to the database's own page for the case, which links the text in six languages.

**1 East African Court of Justice judgment.** *Mohochi v. Uganda* (2013) is the first EACJ judgment on the site. It deals with denial of entry and removal of an EAC citizen under the Common Market Protocol.
