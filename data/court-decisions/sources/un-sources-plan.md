# UN and related databases to mine for "Migration Jurisprudence from the South"

*Plan prepared 11 November 2026, to run from Pranoto's iMac (these sites are blocked from the cloud). Focus: decisions on migrant workers and other non-citizens, in states outside Europe and North America. Gulf entries: doctrinal description only.*

## A. Decisions that become database entries

| # | Database | What it holds | Search for | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **OHCHR Treaty Body Jurisprudence** (juris.ohchr.org) | Views and decisions of all eight committees: Human Rights Committee, CAT, CERD, CEDAW, CESCR, CRC, CRPD, CED | Filter by committee, then by keyword: migrant, alien, expulsion, deportation, domestic worker, trafficking, nationality, residence; then by respondent state outside Europe/North America | Highest. Same format as the Human Rights Committee entries already added |
| 2 | **UNODC SHERLOC case law** (sherloc.unodc.org) | National court decisions on human trafficking and migrant smuggling, many from Asia, Africa and Latin America, with summaries in English | Crime type "trafficking in persons", form "forced labour" or "domestic servitude"; country filters | Highest for national tier. Fills the recruitment-fraud and forced labor situations |
| 3 | **ILO "International labour standards in court decisions"** (ilo.org, compendium) | National court decisions that apply ILO conventions | Migrant workers, forced labour, domestic workers, equality | High. National tier, labor-law angle |
| 4 | **ILO NORMLEX: Committee on Freedom of Association cases and Article 24 representations** | Complaints about union rights and convention compliance, with conclusions | Migrant workers, foreign workers, C97, C143, C29, C189 | High for union and forced labor situations. Quasi-judicial, international tier |
| 5 | **Working Group on Arbitrary Detention opinions** (ohchr.org, opinions database) | Opinions on individual detentions, including immigration detention | Migrant, immigration detention, deportation | Medium-high. Check Gulf entries carefully; keep doctrinal |
| 6 | **International Court of Justice** (icj-cij.org) | Inter-state cases on treatment of foreign nationals | Avena (Mexico v US, 2004) and LaGrand as comparators; Jadhav (India v Pakistan, 2019) as a Southern case on consular access | Low volume, high profile |

## B. Background sources (cite, do not enter as decisions)

| Database | Use |
|---|---|
| **Universal Human Rights Index** (uhri.ohchr.org) | Treaty body, UPR and special procedure recommendations, filterable for "migrants". Good for the "follow-up" field of decision pages |
| **Special Procedures communications** (spcommreports.ohchr.org) | Letters by the Special Rapporteur on migrants and state replies. They name states, so use only as background and keep off the Gulf pages |
| **CMW documents** (treaty body database) | General Comments 1 (domestic workers) and 2 (irregular migrants) and concluding observations. The CMW has no working complaint procedure yet |
| **Refworld** (UNHCR) | Mostly refugee case law; useful to find national decisions cited elsewhere, then verify at the source |

## D. Laws and instruments (for the Migration Law Atlas, not the court decisions page)

| Database | Use |
|---|---|
| **IOM International Migration Law Database** (imldb.iom.int) | International and regional instruments, with some national laws. Check Atlas entries (dates, status, links) and fill gaps. Blocked from the cloud; use the iMac |
| **IOM Key Migration Terms** (iom.int/key-migration-terms), drawn from the IOM *Glossary on Migration* (International Migration Law No. 34, 2019) | Standard definitions (migrant, migrant worker, irregular migration, smuggling, trafficking, etc.). Use to align terms in the explanatory notes and page glossaries, with citation. Blocked from the cloud; save the page or the Glossary PDF via the iMac |
| **Arab Human Rights Committee** (League of Arab States site) | State reports and concluding observations under the Arab Charter, including the 2012–2015 compilation. Background only; keep off the Gulf pages |

## E. Other case collections suggested by Pranoto (11 November 2026)

| Source | Use | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Rights in Exile Programme case law** (rightsinexile.org/case-law/) | National court decisions on refugees, statelessness and detention, with many from Africa and Asia. Good for the national tier; check each decision at the original court source | High |
| **IOM Migration Data Portal** (migrationdataportal.org) | Statistics, not decisions: migrant stocks, remittances, labor migration, recruitment costs (SDG 10.7.1) and the Migration Governance Indicators country profiles. Use for the country context panel and the Countries of Origin notes; take numbers from the original sources it cites | Medium (data pages) |
| **Mobile Pathways immigration court data** (mobilepathways.org) | Dashboards on US immigration court (EOIR) statistics, not individual decisions. Use only as a comparator or context figure; raw data is EOIR Case Data on data.gov or the Deportation Data Project | Low |

## Order of work on the iMac

1. Treaty Body Jurisprudence: export result lists per committee (the site allows CSV/Excel export).
2. SHERLOC: export case lists for trafficking for forced labor and domestic servitude.
3. ILO compendium and NORMLEX CFA cases.
4. WGAD opinions, then ICJ.
5. Regional: Inter-American Commission and ECOWAS Court first (biggest gaps), then EACJ, Caribbean Court of Justice, and top-ups for the African and Inter-American systems.
6. IOM Migration Law Database to check and extend the Migration Law Atlas; Arab Human Rights Committee reports as background.
7. Rights in Exile case law (national decisions from Africa and Asia).
8. Check the 15 unchecked entries added on 11 November 2026 (Qatar v UAE series, four Human Rights Committee views, six advisory opinions).

Each new entry goes into candidates as status C (unchecked), with source URL, and is checked against the official text before it is marked verified.
