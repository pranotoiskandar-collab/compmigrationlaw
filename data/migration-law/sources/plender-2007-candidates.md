# Instruments from Plender, *Basic Documents on International Migration Law* (3rd rev. ed., 2007) not yet in the Migration Law Atlas

I compared the book's table of contents (132 documents) with the 159 entries in the Atlas on 4 November 2026. The book is only a finding list here. The texts should be taken from official free sources such as the UN Treaty Collection, NORMLEX, the Council of Europe, EUR-Lex, OAS and regional secretariats. The book itself is copyrighted (Brill), and the uploaded copy comes from a shadow library.

About 85 of the book's documents are not in the Atlas. Many of them are European, and many have been superseded since 2007. The lists below leave those out.

## Recommended first batch (Global South focus, farm or labor relevance)

| Book no. | Instrument | Tier | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| I.7 | HRC General Comment No. 15, The position of aliens under the ICCPR (1986) | Global | The basic UN text on the rights of non-citizens. It sits alongside CERD GR 30, which the Atlas already has. |
| IX.8 | ILO Protection of Migrant Workers (Underdeveloped Countries) Recommendation, R100 (1955) | Global | It covers seasonal and plantation migration in developing countries and is directly relevant to farmworkers. |
| IX.3 | ILO Recruiting of Indigenous Workers Convention, C50 (1936) | Global | The colonial plantation recruitment regime. It is a historical baseline for the recruitment-fee debate. Its companions C64 and C65 (1939) could be added too. |
| IX.4 | ILO Migration for Employment (Co-operation between States) Recommendation, R62 (1939) | Global | One of the earliest bilateral cooperation standards. |
| IX.1 | ILO Migration Statistics Recommendation, R19 (1922) | Global | The origin of migration statistics standards. |
| IX.5–6 | ILO Social Policy (Non-Metropolitan Territories) Convention C82 (1947) and Labour Standards (Non-Metropolitan Territories) Convention C83 (1947) | Global | Colonial-era rules on migrant labor in plantation economies. |
| IX.10 | ILO Discrimination (Employment and Occupation) Recommendation, R111 (1958) | Global | The companion to C111, which is already in the Atlas. |
| X.6 | North American Agreement on Labor Cooperation (1993) | Regional (Americas) | Its Labor Principle 11 protects migrant workers. It was used in public submissions on H-2A farmworkers. (It was replaced by USMCA chapter 23 in 2020, so record it as historical.) |
| X.4 | Revised Treaty of Chaguaramas, CARICOM (free movement of skilled nationals) | Regional (Americas) | Caribbean free movement. |
| X.1–2 | Caracas Conventions on Territorial Asylum and on Diplomatic Asylum (1954) | Regional (Americas) | The Latin American asylum tradition, alongside the Cartagena Declaration. |
| XI.6–7 | ECOWAS Supplementary Protocols on the right of residence (1986) and the right of establishment (1990) | Regional (Africa) | They complete the 1979 ECOWAS Protocol, which is already in the Atlas. |
| XI.4 | ECOWAS Protocol on the Definition of Community Citizen (1982) | Regional (Africa) | It defines who benefits from free movement. |
| XI.9 | Revised ECOWAS Treaty (1993) | Regional (Africa) | Free movement as a treaty objective. |
| XI.8 | Abuja Treaty establishing the African Economic Community (1991) | Regional (Africa) | The parent treaty of the AU Free Movement Protocol, which is already in the Atlas. |
| XI.10 | SADC Treaty (1992) | Regional (Africa) | The main destination region for labor migration in Southern Africa. The 2005 SADC Protocol on the Facilitation of Movement is not in the book but should go with it. |
| XI.11 | COMESA Treaty (1993) | Regional (Africa) | Free movement in Eastern and Southern Africa. The COMESA Free Movement Protocol (1998) could be added too. |
| XI.5 | ECCAS Treaty (1983) | Regional (Africa) | Free movement in Central Africa. |

## Other global texts worth adding

- UNHCR Statute (GA Res. 428 (V), 1950), III.1
- Declaration on Territorial Asylum (GA Res. 2312 (XXII), 1967), III.6
- Agreement relating to Refugee Seamen (1957) and its 1973 Protocol, III.4–5
- UNHCR Executive Committee Conclusions No. 8, 24, 30, 69, 91, 93 and 99, III.8–14 (soft law)
- Convention on Certain Questions relating to the Conflict of Nationality Laws (Hague, 1930) and its two statelessness protocols, II.1–3
- Convention on the Nationality of Married Women (1957), II.5
- Geneva Convention IV (1949), the articles on aliens in the territory of a party to the conflict, I.2
- Convention against Torture, the full text (the Atlas has Art. 3 only), I.10

## European texts (comparison only; check whether they are still in force)

- Council of Europe: European Convention on Establishment (1955); Social and Medical Assistance (1953); Agreement on the Movement of Persons (1957); Abolition of Visas for Refugees (1959); Au Pair Placement (1969); Repatriation of Minors (1970); Transfer of Responsibility for Refugees (1980); ECHR Protocol No. 7, Art. 1 (expulsion of aliens) and Protocol No. 12; European Social Charter (1961).
- EU (several have been replaced since 2007): the Family Reunification Directive 2003/86/EC and the Long-term Residents Directive 2003/109/EC are still in force and are the most useful. Regulation 1612/68 is now Regulation 492/2011. Dublin II, Eurodac, the Reception and Qualification Directives and the visa regulations have been recast, or replaced by the 2024 Pact, which the Atlas already has.

## Gaps in the book (it stops in 2006)

The book has no ASEAN, GCC or South Asian instruments, and none after 2006. The Atlas already covers C189 (2011), the Global Compact (2018) and the ASEAN Consensus (2017).
