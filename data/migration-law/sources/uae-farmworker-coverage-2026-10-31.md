# UAE: are farmworkers covered by the labour law? (checked 31 Oct 2026)

**Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 (labour relations), Article 3**, quoted from MOHRE's English PDF:

> 1. The provisions hereof shall apply to all establishments, employers and workers in the UAE private sector.
> 2. The following categories shall not be subject to the provisions hereof: a. Employees of federal and local government entities; b. Employees of the armed forces, police and security; and c. Domestic workers.
> 3. The Cabinet may, upon the Minister's proposal, exclude any category from being subject to all or some of the provisions hereof and specify the legislation to be applied thereto.

- Agricultural workers are not excluded. A text search of the Decree-Law and Cabinet Resolution 1/2022 found no mention of "agri", "farm", "shepherd" or "rural".
- The repealed Federal Law 8/1980 is widely reported to have excluded agricultural workers in its Article 3. That claim was not checked against the 1980 text.
- Para. 3 lets the Cabinet exclude further categories. No later Cabinet decision was checked.

**Federal Decree-Law 9/2022 (domestic workers).** The official u.ae portal lists 19 domestic-worker occupations. Among them are farm worker/grower, private agricultural engineer, shepherd and gardener. Farm-type workers employed by a household or private individual therefore fall under 9/2022, not 33/2021. Farm workers employed by a company fall under 33/2021. The law text itself and Cabinet Resolution 106/2022 could not be opened. Amendments in Decree-Laws 20/2023 and 21/2023 were not read.
