# Migration law: incoming

## Jordan (2026-11-18)

`jordan-laws-2026-11-18.csv`: 8 Jordanian laws and regulations on migrant, domestic and farm workers. Before this, the site had almost nothing on Jordan. Every link goes to the official text on the Legislation and Opinion Bureau (lob.gov.jo). The status shown is the status the Bureau gives. Titles are my English translations, with the Arabic title in `title_original`.

These feed several databases: the domestic workers database (Regulations 90/2009 and 63/2020, the 2021 insurance instructions), the farmworker view of the Atlas (Regulations 19/2021 and 4/2003) and the migration law framework (Labour Law, residence, trafficking). All rows are Unverified until an editor places them.

## Regional instruments outside Europe (2026-11-18)

`regional-instruments-2026-11-18.csv` lists 13 regional instruments that were missing from the migration law framework. The framework already had the EU, Council of Europe, ECOWAS, ASEAN, MERCOSUR and Inter-American texts.

| Body | Count | Instruments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| African Union | 5 | African Charter (Art. 12); Children's Charter; Maputo Protocol; 2024 Protocol on nationality and statelessness; 2022 Protocol on social protection |
| East African Community | 1 | Common Market Protocol |
| SADC | 2 | 2005 Movement of Persons Protocol; 2014 Employment and Labour Protocol |
| Andean Community | 4 | Decisions 545, 548, 583 and 878 (Andean Migration Statute, amended by Decision 967 in 2026) |
| Eurasian Economic Union | 1 | 2014 Treaty, labour migration section (the main text for Central Asia) |

Every link goes to the body's own site. Some dates and article numbers come from background knowledge rather than the page; the `note` column says which ones to check. The OAS treaty pages and the League of Arab States sit behind a bot check. They are in the follow-up doc.
