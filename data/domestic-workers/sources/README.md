# Migrant domestic workers: sources not copied here

The database was coded from these documents. They are copyrighted, so they are cited here instead of copied.

- ILO FAIRWAY country profiles: Bahrain (2023), Kuwait (2023), Oman (2023), Qatar (2019 and 2023), Saudi Arabia (2023), United Arab Emirates (2024). ilo.org.
- Zahra, M. (2015). *The legal framework of the sponsorship systems of the Gulf Cooperation Council countries: a comparative examination.* GLMM Explanatory Note 10/2015. gulfmigration.grc.net.
- Vlieger, A. (2012). *Domestic workers in Saudi Arabia and the Emirates: a socio-legal study on conflicts.* Quid Pro Books.

Each coded cell in `../codings.csv` names its source.
