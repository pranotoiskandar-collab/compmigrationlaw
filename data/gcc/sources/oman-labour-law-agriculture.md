# Oman Labour Law (Royal Decree 53/2023) and agricultural workers (iMac check, 11 Nov 2026)
Source: https://qanoon.om/p/2023/rd2023053/
- Art. 2: the law applies to all employers and workers, except those whose work is regulated by special laws or systems. The Minister may regulate "categories with a special nature of work" by decision.
- There is no agricultural exclusion anywhere in the text. A search of the Arabic text for the terms for agriculture, farmer, shepherds and livestock found none.
- So workers on commercial farms are covered by the Labour Law. Farm and livestock workers employed by an individual are a special category under Art. 2, regulated by MD 574/2025: agricultural worker, gardener, camel keeper, livestock keeper and horse groom (MD 574/2025 art. 2).
