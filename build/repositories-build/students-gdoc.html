National court and law repositories: tasks for research assistants

Institute for Migrant Rights, Migration Jurisprudence from the South. Batch 1, 13 November 2026.

What this is

We are listing the official websites where each country publishes its court decisions and its laws on migrant workers (labour, immigration, domestic work, farm work, recruitment, non-discrimination). Claude checked 104 sites. 58 opened normally. The 46 below could not be opened or confirmed automatically, so they need a person.

How to work

  • Write your name in a row before you start it, so two people do not do the same site.
  • In "What you found", give the correct address (if different), whether the site has material on migrant workers (yes / some / no), and the language.
  • Use the sites as an ordinary visitor. Do not create accounts, pay, or get round captchas or bot checks.
  • Use official sites only (courts, governments, parliaments). If you use anything else, label it "secondary".
  • If you find a relevant case, add it to the verification worksheet as usual, or send it to secretariat@theimr.org.
  • Gulf States: neutral wording only.

Search words for court decisions: migrant worker, foreign worker, domestic worker, farm or agricultural worker, recruitment agency, deportation, work permit, wages, trafficking. Use the local-language terms too.
Search words for laws: labour or employment act, immigration or foreigners act, overseas employment or recruitment law, domestic worker rules, anti-discrimination law, latest amendment.

Beware false hits: "migration" can mean digital TV migration or moving staff between job grades. Search for immigration, foreign national, work permit or deportation instead.
