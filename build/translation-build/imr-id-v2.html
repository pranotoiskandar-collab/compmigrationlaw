Draft for Pranoto, 17 November 2026. It rewrites the Indonesian text of the new homepage, the header and menus on every page, and the Legal Clinic's "Where are you now?" steps, following the style of your edits to "data.ijicl.org – terjemahan bahasa Indonesia (draf 2)". Nothing is on the website yet. Once you approve this doc, I'll apply it to the site's language files and send a new Netlify zip.

Rows in bold in the last column are changed. Unchanged rows already fit the style. Article titles, journal names, people's names and addresses stay as they are.

Style rules taken from your edits

  • Speak as "kami". Write "kami kodekan" or "kami periksa" rather than "halaman ini" or a passive di- verb.
  • Use the more formal verb. Use adalah → merupakan, punya → memiliki, lewat → melalui, kebanyakan → sebagian besar, and tak → tidak.
  • Preferred terms. Use referensi → rujukan, metode → cara, data → sumber (when it means the source), neto → bersih, dialog → percakapan, ilmiah → kesarjanaan, Tiongkok → Cina, and Britania Raya → Inggris Raya.
  • Linking words. Use "itu" → "tersebut", a colon or "yaitu" → "yakni", and "bahwa" → "bila" after verbs such as menerima or beranggapan.
  • Full sentences. Split lists after colons and semicolons into sentences. Begin with an explicit link such as "Artinya,", "Namun,", "Selain itu," or "Setelah itu,".
  • English term in brackets after the Indonesian one, where it helps. Examples: "jurnal hukum (law review)" and "pengaduan hak (rights communication)".
  • Reader as "anda", in lower case as in your edits. The site now uses "Anda" with a capital A, the KBBI form. Tell me which to keep across the whole site.

Your earlier site guide (bisa, pengaduan hak, buruh tani, kawasan, ucapan kebencian, Negara asal, Tim, Hubungi Kami) still applies.
