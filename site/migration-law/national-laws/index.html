National laws: other regions

133 national migration, labour and trafficking laws from 25 states in Africa, the Middle East outside the Gulf, East Asia and the Pacific, each with a link to its source. Gathered with AI help from official portals; not yet read by a person.

The Gulf States, East Asia (Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Hong Kong), Southeast Asia and the countries of origin have their own pages with coded comparisons. This table lists laws of other states that the databases cite or that came up in our searches of official portals, so that they can be found and checked. Global and regional instruments are in the Migration Law Atlas.

How to read this table

  • Unverified: the row was found with AI help on the source named and has not yet been read by a person. Titles, years and subjects come from the source listing. See How we use AI.
  • PacLII (secondary source): the Pacific Islands Legal Information Institute (University of the South Pacific) is not a government site. Most Pacific governments publish no searchable database of their own.
  • UNODC SHERLOC (UN-hosted copy): the text as filed with the UN Office on Drugs and Crime legislation database, not the national gazette.
  • Official guidance: a ministry or agency page that explains the law; not the legislation itself.
