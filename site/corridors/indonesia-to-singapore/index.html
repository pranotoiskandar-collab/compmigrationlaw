Court decisions on this corridor

Decisions from courts in Singapore that name Indonesian workers, and decisions from courts in Indonesia that concern work in Singapore (found by text search of the summaries).

  • Parti Liyani v. Public Prosecutor High Court of Singapore · Singapore · 2020 · worker side won

    An Indonesian domestic worker was accused of theft by her employer’s family soon after she was dismissed, and was convicted. On appeal the High Court cleared her completely and said the accusers may have had an improper motive.

Recruitment fees and agencies: Indonesia (6)

  • Decision 20/PUU-XVIII/2020 (bank deposit required of placement companies) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2020 · worker side won

    Directors of two placement companies challenged the bank deposit rule. The Court kept it, as it had in a decision the same day.

  • Decision 83/PUU-XVII/2019 (capital and deposit rules for placement companies) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2020 · worker side won

    The association of placement companies said the high capital and deposit rules of the 2017 law shut small agencies out. A narrow majority upheld the rules, which help make sure agencies can answer for what happens to the workers they send.

  • Decision 12/PUU-XIV/2016 (settling disputes between workers and placement companies) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2017 · worker side lost

    The head of a union of Indonesian overseas workers said the law's dispute rules left workers without a way to court against their placement company. The Court said the rule itself is valid and that any failure lies in how it is applied.

Changing employer: Indonesia (1)

  • Decision 50/PUU-XI/2013 (renewing a contract without first going home) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2014 · worker side won

    Women domestic workers from Cianjur challenged a rule forcing workers with household employers to come home before they could renew their contract. The Court struck the rule down: a protection law should not take away a worker's chance to stay with an employer she wants to keep working for.

Forced labor and trafficking: Singapore (7)

  • Public Prosecutor v. Gaiyathiri d/o Murugayan High Court of Singapore · 2021 · worker side won

    A young domestic worker from Myanmar was starved and beaten to death by her employer in Singapore. The High Court sentenced the employer to life in prison. Others in the household were also prosecuted.

  • Public Prosecutor v. Tay Wee Kiat and Chua Ying Ling High Court of Singapore · 2018 · worker side won

    A couple in Singapore abused their domestic worker from Myanmar for months. On appeal the High Court gave them longer prison terms.

Forced labor and trafficking: Indonesia (1)

  • Decision 32/PUU-XV/2017 (meaning of 'exploitation' in the trafficking law) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2017 · worker side lost

    A man prosecuted under the anti-trafficking law argued that it was unfair to convict without proof of actual exploitation. The Court said the law is clear: the crime is complete when the acts are done for the purpose of exploitation, and his complaint was about the evidence in his own case.

Arrested, detained or expelled: Indonesia (2)

  • Decision 40/PUU-IX/2011 (exit ban during a preliminary investigation) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2012 · worker side won

    Six advocates challenged the power to stop people leaving the country before they are even suspects. The Court agreed and removed it, so a ban on leaving now needs at least a formal criminal investigation.

  • Decision 64/PUU-IX/2011 (repeated extension of exit bans) Constitutional Court of Indonesia · 2012 · worker side won

    A law professor under a travel ban challenged the rule that let the ban be renewed again and again. The Court removed the words 'each time', so renewals now have a limit.
