Country sheets to print and share

One page for each country. Each sheet says, in plain words, what the law of the worker's own country promises before departure and while abroad, lists free help with checked phone numbers, and summarises court decisions that helped workers. Clinics, unions, embassies and families can print them or send them on WhatsApp.

Where the content comes from

The sheets are made automatically from our open data: the Countries of Origin database (law), the Find legal help directory (help, with phone numbers only where checked), and Court Decisions (summaries). The short answers restate the database codes in everyday words; the database pages give the legal sources. This is general information, not legal advice. Last made on 19 November 2026.
