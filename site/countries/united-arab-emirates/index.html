Selected decisions

  • Employer stopped paying salary without formally ending the contract Abu Dhabi Court of Cassation (as reported) · 2025 · worker side won

    A company stopped paying an employee from mid-2025 but never told her in writing that her job had ended. The court said she was still employed and ordered the company to pay her wages, gratuity and leave.

  • Waiver of end-of-service benefits signed during employment Dubai Courts (as reported) · 2025 · worker side won

    An employee had signed a paper giving up end-of-service pay while still working. The court said such a waiver does not count and ordered the benefits paid.

  • Gratuity before one year of service UAE Court of Cassation (as reported) · 2014 · worker side lost

    A worker who left before a year asked for end-of-service pay. The court said the law then in force required a full year. The 2021 law keeps a one-year threshold. [Check.]
