Injury, illness or death at work: migrant worker court decisions in Philippines

Court decisions from Philippines on compensation for migrant workers injured, ill or killed at work: 467 court decisions (1 full summaries, 466 unchecked candidates). Claims for disability and death benefits, medical costs and compensation, including seafarers’ claims under standard employment contracts and claims against insurers or welfare funds in the country of origin.

Relevant standards: ILO Equality of Treatment (Accident Compensation) Convention, 1925 (No. 19); ILO Maritime Labour Convention, 2006

Decisions

  • Becmen Service Exporter v. Cuaresma Supreme Court of the Philippines · 2009 · worker side won

    A young Filipina nurse died in Saudi Arabia and the employer called it suicide. Her parents sued. The Supreme Court held the recruitment agency and the employer liable to the family.
