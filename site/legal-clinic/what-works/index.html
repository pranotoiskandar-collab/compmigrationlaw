South AfricaAfrika Selatan

18 coded decisions in the collection18 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

Most rights in the Bill of Rights belong to "everyone", and the Constitutional Court has read that word to include people without South African citizenship. Equality arguments therefore succeed often: permanent residents could not be barred from teaching posts or from social grants. Labour courts have held that a worker without a valid permit is still an employee. Asylum seekers have won a right to work. Public interest organisations bring many of these cases, and the Court can order structural remedies. The limits appear where a job is tied to state security or to the legal profession, where citizenship requirements have been upheld.Sebagian besar hak dalam Bill of Rights dimiliki "setiap orang", dan Mahkamah Konstitusi membaca kata itu mencakup orang yang bukan warga negara Afrika Selatan. Karena itu argumen kesetaraan sering berhasil: penduduk tetap tidak boleh dihalangi menjadi guru atau menerima bantuan sosial. Pengadilan perburuhan memutuskan bahwa pekerja tanpa izin yang sah tetap seorang pekerja. Pencari suaka memperoleh hak untuk bekerja. Banyak perkara ini diajukan organisasi kepentingan publik, dan Mahkamah bisa memerintahkan pemulihan struktural. Batasnya terlihat pada pekerjaan yang terkait keamanan atau profesi hukum, di mana syarat kewarganegaraan dipertahankan.

Hong KongHong Kong

14 coded decisions in the collection14 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

Hong Kong combines the common law with the Basic Law and a Bill of Rights, but the Bill of Rights Ordinance carries a reservation for immigration matters, and the courts give the government wide room on who may stay. Arguments that foreign domestic helpers should be treated like other residents, for permanent residence or against the live-in rule, have failed. What works better is the criminal law against abusive employers, review of detention, and the torture-screening procedure before removal. Welfare rules that excluded new residents have been struck down where they were disproportionate.Hong Kong memadukan common law dengan Basic Law dan Bill of Rights, tetapi Bill of Rights Ordinance memuat reservasi untuk urusan imigrasi, dan pengadilan memberi pemerintah keleluasaan luas tentang siapa yang boleh tinggal. Argumen agar pekerja rumah tangga asing diperlakukan seperti penduduk lain, untuk izin tinggal tetap atau melawan kewajiban tinggal di rumah majikan, gagal. Yang lebih berhasil adalah hukum pidana terhadap majikan yang kejam, peninjauan penahanan, dan prosedur penyaringan risiko penyiksaan sebelum pemulangan. Aturan bantuan sosial yang mengecualikan penduduk baru dibatalkan bila tidak proporsional.

IndiaIndia

14 coded decisions in the collection14 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

The Supreme Court reads the right to life in Article 21 broadly and applies it to every person, citizen or not. Public interest litigation lets organisations, and even a letter, start a case on behalf of bonded or migrant labourers. The Court then issues detailed directions and keeps supervising them, as in the bonded labour cases and the 2021 orders on rations and registration for migrant workers. Foreigners, however, have no constitutional right to reside, so challenges to deportation have usually failed.Mahkamah Agung membaca hak atas hidup dalam Pasal 21 secara luas dan menerapkannya pada setiap orang, warga negara atau bukan. Litigasi kepentingan publik memungkinkan organisasi, bahkan sepucuk surat, memulai perkara atas nama buruh ijon atau buruh migran. Mahkamah lalu mengeluarkan perintah rinci dan terus mengawasinya, seperti dalam perkara kerja ijon dan perintah tahun 2021 tentang jatah pangan dan pendaftaran buruh migran. Namun orang asing tidak memiliki hak konstitusional untuk menetap, sehingga gugatan terhadap deportasi umumnya gagal.

PhilippinesFilipina

12 coded decisions in the collection12 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

The Philippines is a country of origin whose courts discipline the recruitment industry. The Constitution promises full protection to labour at home and overseas, and the Migrant Workers Act makes the local agency liable jointly and severally with the employer abroad. The Supreme Court applies that rule consistently, and it struck down, twice, a cap on the money owed to workers dismissed without cause. Bans on women's deployment have been upheld as an exercise of police power, so equality arguments against exit restrictions have not worked here.Filipina adalah negara asal yang pengadilannya mendisiplinkan industri penempatan. Konstitusi menjanjikan perlindungan penuh bagi pekerja di dalam dan luar negeri, dan Undang-Undang Pekerja Migran membuat agen lokal bertanggung jawab tanggung renteng dengan majikan di luar negeri. Mahkamah Agung menerapkan aturan itu secara konsisten, dan dua kali membatalkan batas uang yang wajib dibayar kepada pekerja yang diberhentikan tanpa alasan. Larangan penempatan perempuan dipertahankan sebagai wewenang negara, sehingga argumen kesetaraan melawan pembatasan keberangkatan tidak berhasil di sini.

CanadaKanada

11 coded decisions in the collection11 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

The Charter protects "everyone" in its core guarantees, and the Supreme Court treats non-citizenship as a ground of discrimination. Refugee claimants won a right to a fair hearing, and the exclusion of farmworkers from collective bargaining was struck down, although a later, weaker scheme was upheld. Much of the success comes through administrative law, where officers must weigh the best interests of children in humanitarian decisions. Southeast Asia (Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia)Asia Tenggara (Malaysia, Singapura, Thailand, Indonesia)

11 coded decisions in the collection11 putusan berkode dalam koleksi

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

Few constitutional rulings on migrant workers exist in this region. Most decisions in the collection come from criminal courts, where employers who abused domestic workers were convicted in Malaysia and Singapore, though not in every case. They also come from labour and administrative courts, such as Thailand's order to compensate Myanmar workers and a Malaysian award against a recruiter. Defamation law has been used against advocates who expose abuse. Iskandar (2018) traces the weakness of rights-based arguments to the region's sharp line between legal and illegal foreigners and to the consensus style of ASEAN. He points to national human rights institutions such as SUHAKAM as a home-grown route. In Indonesia, the Constitutional Court kept fishers and ship crew inside the migrant worker protection law. Lyon and Iskandar (2024) show that workers reach the law mainly through trusted intermediaries.Di kawasan ini hanya ada sedikit putusan konstitusional tentang pekerja migran. Sebagian besar putusan dalam koleksi berasal dari pengadilan pidana, tempat majikan yang menganiaya pekerja rumah tangga dihukum di Malaysia dan Singapura, meski tidak dalam setiap kasus. Putusan lain berasal dari pengadilan perburuhan dan tata usaha negara, seperti perintah Thailand untuk membayar kompensasi kepada pekerja Myanmar dan putusan Malaysia terhadap agen perekrut. Hukum pencemaran nama baik pernah dipakai terhadap pembela yang membongkar pelanggaran. Iskandar (2018) menelusuri lemahnya argumen berbasis hak pada garis tajam antara orang asing legal dan ilegal di kawasan ini serta pada gaya musyawarah ASEAN. Ia menunjuk lembaga HAM nasional seperti SUHAKAM sebagai jalur yang tumbuh dari dalam. Di Indonesia, Mahkamah Konstitusi mempertahankan awak kapal dan nelayan dalam UU pelindungan pekerja migran. Lyon dan Iskandar (2024) menunjukkan bahwa pekerja menjangkau hukum terutama lewat perantara yang mereka percaya.

What makes it distinctiveApa yang membuatnya khas Being checkedSedang diperiksa

The decisions in the collection from the Gulf States are mostly wage and contract claims in labour courts and courts of cassation. Workers often won these claims, usually on the written terms of the contract and on payment records. A claim normally begins with a complaint to the labour ministry, which tries to settle it before referring it to court. In Kuwait, the Constitutional Court upheld the Domestic Workers Law when recruitment offices challenged it. Practical points for these courts are good documentation and early use of the ministry procedure.Putusan dari Negara Teluk dalam koleksi ini sebagian besar adalah gugatan upah dan kontrak di pengadilan perburuhan dan pengadilan kasasi. Pekerja sering memenangkan gugatan ini, biasanya berdasarkan isi kontrak tertulis dan catatan pembayaran. Gugatan biasanya dimulai dengan pengaduan ke kementerian ketenagakerjaan, yang berupaya mendamaikan sebelum meneruskannya ke pengadilan. Di Kuwait, Mahkamah Konstitusi mempertahankan Undang-Undang Pekerja Rumah Tangga ketika digugat kantor-kantor perekrutan. How this guide is madeCara panduan ini disusun

The guide is generated from the Court Decisions data (version 0.1, 2026-11-01). It uses only decisions with a written summary, not unchecked candidates. Each argument a court ruled on is coded as accepted or rejected, and each decision is tagged with the reasoning tools the court relied on, following Farnsworth's The Tools of Argument and the other sources listed in the Court Decisions method section. The comparisons with the rest of the collection flag differences of 35 percentage points or more, and only where the argument was tried at least twice in the jurisdiction and three times elsewhere. They describe this collection only. The distinctive-feature notes were written with AI assistance from the decisions and from the sources named in each note, and they are being checked. Corrections are welcome at secretariat@theimr.org.Panduan ini dibuat dari data Putusan Pengadilan (versi 0.1, 2026-11-01). Panduan ini hanya memakai putusan yang sudah memiliki ringkasan tertulis, bukan kandidat yang belum diperiksa. Setiap argumen yang diputus pengadilan dikodekan sebagai diterima atau ditolak, dan setiap putusan ditandai dengan alat penalaran yang dipakai pengadilan, mengikuti The Tools of Argument karya Farnsworth dan sumber lain yang tercantum di bagian metode Putusan Pengadilan. Perbandingan ini hanya menggambarkan koleksi ini. Catatan tentang ciri khas ditulis dengan bantuan AI dari putusan dan dari sumber yang disebut dalam setiap catatan, dan sedang diperiksa.
