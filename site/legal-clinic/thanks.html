What happens next

  1. 1
    An officer reads your draft

    An IMR Clinic officer reads what you sent and listens to any voice message.

  2. 2
    We contact you

    The officer checks the facts with you, asks for any documents, and confirms that you agree to go ahead.

  3. 3
    We decide together

    You and the officer choose whether to file, and with which body. Nothing is sent without your agreement.

  4. 4
    Filing and waiting

    After filing, a UN committee often takes three to five years to decide a case. An urgent request, such as stopping a deportation, can be answered within days. The Clinic tells you at each step what has happened.

Want to know how these bodies work? Read the ISHR guide to UN complaints. If you have already returned home and are owed wages, see Getting your rights after you return.
