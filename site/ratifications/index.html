Ratification tracker

Which states have joined the five treaties written for migrant workers: the ILO Migration for Employment Convention (C97), the ILO Migrant Workers Convention (C143), the ILO Domestic Workers Convention (C189), the ILO Violence and Harassment Convention (C190), and the UN Migrant Workers Convention (ICRMW).

The table is refreshed every week from the official records: ILO NORMLEX for the four ILO conventions and the UN Treaty Collection for the ICRMW. The date each record was last checked is shown below the table.

The five treaties

TreatyWhat it does for migrant workersOfficial record
C97 (1949)Equal treatment with nationals on pay, union rights, housing, social security and access to courts for migrants who are lawfully in the country.NORMLEX
C143 (1975)Basic human rights for all migrant workers, including those without papers; action against clandestine movements and illegal employment; equal opportunity.NORMLEX
C189 (2011)Domestic workers get the labor rights other workers have: a written contract, rest days, a minimum wage, and the right to keep their own documents.NORMLEX
C190 (2019)The right to a world of work free from violence and harassment, for everyone who works, whatever their contract or status.NORMLEX
ICRMW (1990)The UN treaty on the rights of all migrant workers and their families, with or without papers, from recruitment to return.UN Treaty Collection

How to read and cite this

An ILO convention binds a state twelve months after the ILO registers its ratification; until then it is shown as "ratified, not yet in force". The ICRMW has been in force since 1 July 2003, so it binds each party from the date it ratified or acceded. A signature alone creates no obligation to apply the treaty, only an obligation not to defeat its purpose. Where an official record and this table differ, the official record is right; please tell us at secretariat@theimr.org.

Cite as: Institute for Migrant Rights, Ratification tracker (theimr.org/ratifications/, accessed [date]), compiled from ILO NORMLEX and the UN Treaty Collection.

Konvensi ILO mengikat suatu negara dua belas bulan setelah ILO mendaftarkan ratifikasinya; sebelum itu, kami tampilkan sebagai "diratifikasi, belum berlaku". ICRMW telah berlaku sejak 1 Juli 2003, sehingga mengikat setiap negara pihak sejak tanggal ratifikasi atau aksesinya. Penandatanganan saja tidak menimbulkan kewajiban untuk menerapkan perjanjian tersebut, melainkan hanya kewajiban untuk tidak menggagalkan tujuannya.
