Volunteer

Work with us

IMR is a small team. Volunteers help us collect, check and translate migration law so that migrant workers and the people who help them can use it. Tell us what you can do, and we will reply with a first task that fits.

Four ways to help

  • 1. Spread and spotShare our pages, report a broken link, flag a new law or court ruling you read about. Ten minutes at a time.
  • 2. Translate and reviewCheck our Indonesian, Arabic, Bengali, Dari, Nepali, Tagalog or Vietnamese pages, or start a new language. A second speaker checks every translation.
  • 3. Legal researchFind the official text of a judgment or law, confirm the details, and record the link. Law students and lawyers welcome.
  • 4. Data and codeSecond coding of our law codings (Cohen's kappa), data checks, scrapers for court websites, and statistics.

Sign up

It takes about three minutes. We reply by email within two weeks.
